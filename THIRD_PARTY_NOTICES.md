The optional workspace-local location runtime installs:

- `pymobiledevice3` 10.7.4, licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.

It is installed from PyPI into `.runtime/venv` and invoked as a separate command-line program. Its source, license, and dependency metadata are available from:

https://github.com/doronz88/pymobiledevice3

Deleting `.runtime/` removes the installed runtime and its local caches.
